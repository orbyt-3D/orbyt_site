Changelog

What changed in orbyt and on this site.

0.9.2 preview 12 Nov 2026

  • orbyt3d.space launched, hosted on Vercel with HTTPS and strict security headers.
  • Live showcase page: orbit the launch site, server hall and wind farm scenes in the browser with an fps and draw-call readout.
  • Docs: getting started, models and agents (MCP tools), performance and memory.
  • Landing hero rendering ~2× faster: static meshes merged per material, one-time shadow map, GPU particle steam.
  • Engine 0.9.2 built and tested: Flare (real-time), Bake (path tracer), glTF and HDRI addons, and orbyt-mcp with 18 tools. npm release coming soon.
  • Honest numbers on the landing page: orbyt adds 3.5 KB gzip on top of three.js; features not in 0.9.2 yet are marked “soon”.
